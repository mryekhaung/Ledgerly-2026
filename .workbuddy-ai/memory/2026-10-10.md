### Decisions
- Money colors: **out = expense = red/coral, in = income = green/sage**, universal and NOT
  configurable (documented in Design.md §2.4).
- Design tokens split into a `AppColors` interface (not just ThemeData) so semantic names
  (moneyIn, surfaceSunken) are readable in widgets and light/dark is swappable in tests.
- Riverpod `legacy.dart` ban is enforced by a CI grep job, not a lint (no import-ban lint exists).
- Did NOT declare drift/dio/freezed in pubspec yet — added per-roadmap-item, not upfront.
